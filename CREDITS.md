## Third-party assets

### Superpowers Asset Packs — CC0 1.0 (public domain)
Created by [Pixel-boy](https://twitter.com/2pblog1) for
[Superpowers](http://superpowers-html5.com/).

Released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** — free for commercial and non-commercial
use, no attribution required (credited here anyway).

- **Medieval Fantasy Asset Pack** — terrain tiles, world props, overworld
  monsters, item icons, HUD pieces, and the music and sound effects.
- **Western FPS 2D Asset Pack** — additional 32×32 terrain tiles used for the
  dungeon and stone environments.
- **RPG Battle System Asset Pack** — 48 battle backdrops, 6 hero battler sheets,
  11 enemy battler sheets, HUD bars, icons, and the bitmap fonts.
- **Top-Down Shooter Asset Pack** — reference for the modular character
  proportions used by the character compositor.

Repo: <https://github.com/sparklinlabs/superpowers-asset-packs>

### Super Pixel Effects Gigapack (Free Version)

Free for commercial and non-commercial use in games. **Attribution required and
given here.** Used for spell, impact, portal and weather effects.

| `32rogues` | Its license forbids use in generative-AI-associated projects. |
| `Cute Fantasy Free` | Non-commercial **and** no-redistribution — incompatible with a public repository. |
| `Mana Seed Farmer Sprite` | Free sample of a paid pack; redistribution terms unclear. |
| 3D / vehicle / space-shooter / western FPS art | Wrong genre. |

## Fonts

In-game UI uses a bitmap-style font sheet from the RPG Battle System pack (CC0).
The title and lore screens use the browser's default serif/monospace stacks
(`Georgia`, `ui-monospace`) so no additional font licensing is introduced.
